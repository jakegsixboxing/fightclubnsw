What Is Fight Club

A team, not just a training block

At its heart, Fight Club is a big group of everyday local people training together, in-house, at Legacy Gym on the Central Coast — pushing together, sparring together, and fighting together on the same fight night.

Members consistently say it's done as much for their mental health, physical health, and self-confidence as it has for their boxing — Fight Club has helped plenty of people on the Central Coast find structure and confidence again, including some walking away from alcohol and drugs for good. For the vast majority of graduates, that's the whole point: ticking off the ultimate bucket-list challenge and finding out what they're really made of. A small few choose to keep going afterwards, into Fight Club's amateur pathway (coming soon). Fight Club has been running on the Central Coast for over ten years — previously as Red vs Blue, rebranded to Fight Club in 2025 — and over 1,000 people Central Coast–wide have now completed the Challenge.

How Training Works

One team first. Then your team.

Fight Club runs in two phases across the ten weeks — one shared foundation, then sharper, smaller teams under your own coach.

Phase 01 — Weeks 1–5

One Team

Every fighter trains together as one big group — building fundamentals, footwork, and a shared sense of community from day one. This is where the team is really built.

Phase 02 — Weeks 5–10

Team Split

At the five-week mark, the group breaks into two or three smaller teams, each sharpening the specific skills and drills their coach decides they need. Everyone still comes back together each week for technical and open sparring.

The Weekly Grind

A week inside Fight Club

Monday Tech Sparring

Light-to-moderate sparring broken into specific skill and drill work, targeting exactly what each fighter needs to improve.

Tuesday 6:45pm — Skills & Drills

A participants-only session in a private area of the gym — attacking, defence, footwork, ring awareness, and more.

Wednesday Open Sparring

Full open rounds — putting it all into play. Later in the program, sparring is pre-matched days in advance, so you know exactly who, how many rounds, and which ring.

Thursday 6:45pm — Skills & Drills

A second private session each week, keeping the fundamentals sharp alongside your team.

Train at Your Own Gym

Not near the Central Coast? Complete the Challenge through any gym in NSW under the ABL. Your day-to-day training stays with your own coach and team. Throughout the ten weeks you'll take part in communal sparring days and competition sparring events so we can find your best possible match — training out of Sydney or Newcastle instead, we bring a Fight Club rep to a local sparring day to watch and film you, and you can upload sparring footage through the app too. We strongly encourage getting to the Central Coast at least once during the ten weeks for your final matchup. You'll also need to register with the NSW Combat Sports Authority for your Blue Book, complete your medical and bloodwork, and register with the ABL.

The Journey

The 10-Week Challenge

Once you're matched to a fight night, the countdown starts. Ten structured weeks turn a sign-up into a fight-ready athlete — this is what makes Fight Club feel like a lot more than a gym membership.

Week 1 — Challenge Begins

Your profile is locked in and training starts

Your fighter profile, coach, and gym are locked in, and your 10-week countdown to fight night starts. Your match isn't set yet — that gets built up from real sparring data as the weeks go on.

Weeks 2–4 — Foundation

Conditioning and challenge fundamentals

Early weeks build the base: conditioning, technique, and weight tracking, with your coach setting the training blocks around your fight date. The first communal sparring days start around here too, feeding real data into your eventual match.

Weeks 5–8 — Sharpen

Now split into your team

Fresh off the five-week mark, you're now training in your smaller team under your coach — sharpening the specific skills they've identified for you. The whole group still comes back together each week for technical and open sparring, so matches keep sharpening toward your fairest possible pairing. Training out of Sydney or Newcastle? A Fight Club rep comes to a local sparring day to watch and film you instead, and you can upload sparring footage through the app too.

Week 9 — Fight Week Prep

Taper and final checks

Training tapers, weight is finalised, and your fight card details — including your matched opponent — are confirmed.

Week 10 — Fight Night

You step in the ring

Your first official amateur bout, in front of a live crowd at our fully custom fight venue — registered with the NSW Combat Sports Authority and the ABL.

Training at your own gym? Your day-to-day sessions stay with your own coach and team throughout — the sparring days (or your local Sydney/Newcastle sparring day) are for matchmaking only. We strongly encourage getting to the Central Coast at least once during the ten weeks for your final matchup.
